## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-polished flagship retail environment inspired by minimalist technological craftsmanship. Built for high-end consumer hardware, ecosystem devices, and accessories, the aesthetic balances the surgical restraint of contemporary industrial design with an inviting, tactile digital shopping journey.

### Design Principles
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Whitespace is not empty space; it is structural architecture. Layouts prioritize breathing room, low-contrast subtle surfaces, and delicate hairline boundaries over cluttered decoration.
- **Architectural Typography:** Headlines in Manrope introduce crisp geometric certainty, while Be Vietnam Pro provides flawless, legible, and balanced Vietnamese typesetting across technical descriptions and metadata.
- **Restrained Commercialism:** Price tags and product values do not scream in loud promotional hues; they command attention through solid contrast and tabular precision. Semantic alerts are deployed strictly when actionable.
- **Fluid Precision:** Transitions feel weighted and natural, mirroring physical anodized aluminum, glass, and ceramic finishes.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, calm retail environment. Surfaces rely primarily on pure `#FFFFFF` canvas zones paired with `#F5F5F7` subtle fills to frame hardware imagery without chromatic competition.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#0055D6` / `#0A6CFF`):** Reserved strictly for focal interactive drivers: checkout progression, primary purchasing CTAs, active segmented toggles, and critical touch targets.
- **Neutral Core (`#1D1D1F`):** Anchors high-contrast typographic hierarchy. Primary prices intentionally reuse `#1D1D1F` rather than loud discount colors to maintain high-end credibility.
- **Subtle Backdrops (`#F5F5F7`):** Frames product photography, thumbnail containers, input surfaces, and technical specification bands.
- **Structural Lines (`#E3E3E8`):** Restrained 1px structural outlines separating distinct modules without creating visual clutter.
- **Semantics:** `#16A05A` highlights guaranteed stock availability; `#D97706` notifies low inventory (&lt; 5 items); `#D92D20` isolates urgent validation errors or stock depletion.

## Typography

The typographic architecture combines Manrope for geometric structural headlines and Be Vietnam Pro for nuanced, stress-free microcopy and body passages.

### Formatting Rules
- **Vietnamese Currency:** All pricing strings must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate jitter across variable digits and tables. Standard format strictly adheres to Vietnamese convention: dot-separated thousands followed by the currency unit (e.g., `29.990.000₫`).
- **Headlines:** Display titles and section intros use Manrope with tighter letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to mirror luxury editorial spreads.
- **Technical Specs:** Tabular values in comparison sheets use `body-sm` set in Be Vietnam Pro, maintaining strict vertical alignment and clean vertical baseline grids.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8-point base rhythm, deploying generous vertical breathing spaces between high-profile product showcases.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column layout on Desktop (1024px+) with a fixed 1280px maximum content container. 8 columns on Tablet (640px–1023px) and 4 columns on Mobile (&lt; 640px).
- **Section Breaks:** Flagship landing pages transition between sections using large vertical spans (`space-2xl` / 80px), collapsing to `48px` on mobile viewports.
- **Edge Distances:** Maintain `margin` boundaries of 32px on desktop and 16px along mobile borders to prevent elements touching display edges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages subtle tonal shifts, glassmorphic backdrops, and diffused, atmospheric shadows rather than heavy structural borders.

### Surface Elevation Levels
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and `#F5F5F7` surfaces sit flush. Cards sit borderless over alternating subtle section containers or defined with 1px hairline `#E3E3E8`.
- **Raised Interactive (Level 1):** Hovered product cards lift slightly using an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04), 0 8px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Overlays (Level 2):** Autocomplete search panels, persistent bottom cart summary bars, and context menus utilize `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` paired with an ultra-fine border.
- **Modals & Hardware Drawers (Level 3):** Product configuration sheets and drawers implement `0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14)`.
- **Material Translucency:** The sticky global navigation uses `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` over an 85% opacity `#FFFFFF` background, allowing device silhouettes to diffuse naturally as users scroll.

## Shapes

The design system uses balanced curvature to evoke industrial hardware finishes.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Inputs & Status Badges:** Base roundedness (`0.5rem` / 8px) creates clean form controls, capacity tags, and stock indicators.
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** Intermediate roundedness (12px) provides a tactile, smooth tap target.
- **Product & Content Cards:** Enclosed containers apply `rounded-lg` (16px) to frame device images smoothly.
- **Hero Windows & Drawers:** High-impact banners and slide-over side panels feature `rounded-xl` (24px).
- **Pill Elements:** Interactive color selectors, full-spectrum spec swatches, and primary floating action buttons use fully circular geometry (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** 48px height (large) or 40px (default). Background `#0055D6`, text `#FFFFFF`, font Manrope 600. Smooth hover transitions to `#0757D1` over 150ms. Corner radius 12px.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Subtle `#F5F5F7` fill, text `#1D1D1F`, hover `#E3E3E8`. For outlines, use 1px `#C7C7CF` with clear inner content.
- **Touch Target:** Guarantee minimum 44px hit bounds on touch devices.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in 16px rounded borders with hairline `#E3E3E8` or sitting on a flush `#F5F5F7` background container.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:3, centered with ample internal padding (24px). Smooth image scaling to 1.03 on desktop pointer hover.
- **Typography & Pricing:** Device title in Manrope 600 (`body-base`), tabular price display in `#1D1D1F` (`price-md`), and crossed-out historical price in `#8E8E96` (`body-sm`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Compact 4px vertical by 8px horizontal padding, 8px radius. Use 10-12% opacity tints for semantic backgrounds paired with high-contrast text.
- **Capacity / Spec Selectors:** 1px `#E3E3E8` border, 8px radius. Active selection state displays a solid 2px `#0055D6` outline with matching bold text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Input Fields:** 44px uniform height, 8px radius, `#F5F5F7` background fill, and subtle 1px border on blur.
- **Focus State:** 2px focus ring in `#0055D6` with a 2px offset. Placeholder text in `#8E8E96`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** 20px diameter/width, 1px border. Checked state fills with `#0055D6` bearing a pure white tick or center dot.

### Color Swatches
- **Hardware Palette Selector:** 28px circular disks displaying actual device anodization tones, enclosed in a 2px white gap and external brand ring when active.
